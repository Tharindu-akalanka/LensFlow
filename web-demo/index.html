SNAPSORT IN YOUR BROWSER

Try the workflow.

This demo runs entirely in your browser. Imported files are read locally, downscaled for review and held in memory. They are not uploaded or saved to a server. Reloading or closing this tab clears your shoot.

Sharpness, exposure and perceptual similarity are simple browser heuristics. The desktop app has a separate Python analysis engine, RAW support, persistent projects and a Lightroom Classic plug-in. This demo does not run those models, detect eyes/faces or connect to Lightroom.
